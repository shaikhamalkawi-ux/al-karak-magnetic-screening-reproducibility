# Data availability and reuse terms — Al-Karak numerical site dataset

The curated historical **numerical site-level dataset**, data dictionary, and source-to-aggregate verification materials in `data/` are released for public scientific access and attribution-based reuse by the project research contributors, under the author-reported approval to disseminate these numerical measurements.

Recommended attribution: Lataifeh, M. S., and El-Hasan, T. M. (2009–2011), Al-Karak environmental magnetism project; El-Hasan, T., and Lataifeh, M. S. (2013), *Environmental Earth Sciences*, DOI 10.1007/s12665-012-2058-4; and the present reanalysis by the five named authors (2026; pre-submission).

This permission concerns **the curated public numerical CSV and explanatory documentation**, not the historical project’s unposted original instrument spreadsheets, private personal-site notes, or third-party mapping material. The numerical measurements are supplied **as historically recorded** with no warranty of original instrument calibration.

For maximal interoperability, the source data are published with the terms of **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** for this curated CSV and data dictionary: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The project authors should be credited and changes to the dataset should be indicated.
